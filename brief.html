Geopolitics

Iran diplomacy, Xi summit, Ukraine backdrop

Iran remains the other flashpoint. The Strait of Hormuz and the conflict in the Middle East were under focus at the United Nations General Assembly this week, and Trump told the U.N. he has a "big decision" over whether to reach a deal with Tehran or "annihilate" the country, even as lower-level talks reportedly continued.

The Fed and rates

Yields at 19-year highs, hike odds rising

The bond market, not stocks, has been the story this week. The 10-year Treasury yield jumped to 5.11%, its highest level since 2007, while the 30-year yield rose to a post-2004 peak of 5.438%, pressured by strong economic data and a weak Treasury auction.

Under new Chair Kevin Warsh, the Fed has already hiked once this cycle, and futures markets now lean toward another move. At the start of 2026 markets were pricing in cuts, but traders are now pricing an additional hike this year that would bring the overnight rate to 4.00%-4.25%, according to iShares' fixed-income outlook.

Odds vary by source: Polymarket's dashboard puts the October 29 FOMC decision at 54% for a 25bp hike versus 46% for no change, while one markets newsletter cited Fed funds futures pricing a 73.5 percent probability of another hike at the October 28 meeting — a reminder that these estimates move fast and differ by methodology.

AI and tech

Blowout earnings meet political pushback

The fundamentals still look strong: Nvidia reported second-quarter revenue of $96.2 billion, up 106% from a year ago, with Data Center revenue of $89.0 billion, up 117%, and CEO Jensen Huang said demand is accelerating.

But politics is becoming a real variable for the AI trade. A Bloomberg analysis found 61% of U.S. adults now oppose new data centers in their communities, a 12-point increase, and 75 planned projects worth a combined $30 billion have been halted as the midterms approach.

That opposition is starting to show up in how Wall Street frames the sector: Barclays and Bank of America have begun explicitly factoring this political risk into their trade recommendations for AI stocks for the first time, according to reporting on the shift.
